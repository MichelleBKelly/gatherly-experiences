WHAT IS GATHERLY?

Bringing the fun of gathering together,
wherever you are.

Gatherly is a virtual experience platform that brings people together to enjoy the fun of shared experiences, no matter the distance. Through interactive games, activities, and group experiences, we recreate the excitement of getting together in person in a virtual setting.

Whether it's team building with coworkers, celebrating with friends, or spending time with family, Gatherly makes it easy to share experiences, have fun, and create memorable moments from anywhere.

We believe connection shouldn't depend on being in the same room. Gatherly brings people together through fun, interactive virtual experiences that turn screen time into quality time.

OUR STORY

Built for togetherness,
wherever you are.

Connection is what
brings us together.

Gatherly was created around a simple idea: some of the best moments happen when people come together.

Whether it's coworkers building stronger relationships, friends celebrating a special occasion, or families staying connected across distances, shared experiences bring people closer.

As more of our lives move online, we wanted to make virtual gatherings feel less like another video call and more like something people genuinely look forward to. Gatherly brings people together through interactive games, creative activities, and hosted experiences that make connecting virtually easy, engaging, and fun.
